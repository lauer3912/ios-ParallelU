# ParallelU — Feature List

> **Target Users:** Gen-Z, Millennials, Gen-X in US/EU markets
> **App Concept:** Social app where users create alternate versions of themselves (parallel universe alter-egos) and interact with others' parallel selves

## Core Features (60+ Total)

### 🚀 1. Parallel Self Creation (12 features)
1. **Life Path Selector** — Swipe to choose life dimensions: Career, Family, Creativity, Adventure, Finance, Health
2. **Appearance Builder** — Customize avatar face shape, skin tone, hair, accessories
3. **Personality Quiz** — 10-question quiz to auto-generate parallel self personality
4. **Universe Background** — Select cosmic background themes for each parallel self
5. **Power Words** — Choose 3 signature words that define this version of yourself
6. **Life Timeline** — Visual timeline showing key decisions in this universe
7. **Alternate History** — Write a short bio of "what changed" in this universe
8. **Emotion Palette** — Select mood/personality traits (3 max)
9. **Talent Assignment** — Assign 2 talents this universe version excels at
10. **Memory Fragments** — Upload/describe one "memory" from this universe
11. **Clone Original** — Start from current real-life profile as base
12. **Universe Naming** — Name your parallel universe (e.g., "Universe-A")

### 🌌 2. Home Feed & Discovery (10 features)
13. **Parallel Feed** — See posts from your parallel selves
14. **Discovery Radar** — Find others' parallel selves by universe type
15. **Universe Map** — Visual constellation map of parallel universes
16. **Trending Parallels** — Most popular parallel selves this week
17. **Near Universe** — Find parallels with similar life paths nearby
18. **Universe Filters** — Filter by life dimension (Career/Family/etc)
19. **Swipe Match** — Swipe right on parallel selves you resonate with
20. **Feed Reactions** — React with universe-themed emojis (🌌✨🔮💫)
21. **Save to Collection** — Bookmark interesting parallels
22. **Share Profile** — Generate shareable card of your parallel self

### 💌 3. Time Capsule / Messages (8 features)
23. **Capsule Creation** — Write a message to your parallel self
24. **Capsule Unlock Date** — Set when the message can be opened (1 day to 1 year)
25. **Capsule Privacy** — Public, friends only, or private capsules
26. **Capsule Responses** — Receive responses from parallel versions
27. **Capsule Gallery** — View all your capsules (sent/received)
28. **Cross-Universe Mail** — Send actual letters between universes
29. **Memory Box** — Store "artifact" images representing universe memories
30. **Capsule Reminders** — Notification when capsule is about to unlock

### 👥 4. Social & Interaction (10 features)
31. **Parallel Chat** — Chat with your parallel selves or others' parallels
32. **Universe Debate** — Start debates: "Which universe made better choice?"
33. **Choice Polls** — Create polls: "What would your parallel self choose?"
34. **Story Continuation** — Collaborative story writing across universes
35. **Universe Badges** — Earn badges for universe achievements
36. **Friend Requests** — Connect with real people and their parallels
37. **Blocked Users** — Block/report inappropriate content
38. **Online Status** — See when parallels are active
39. **Typing Indicator** — See when someone is typing
40. **Read Receipts** — Optional read receipts for DMs

### 🎮 5. Gamification (8 features)
41. **Universe Points** — Earn points for creating content and interacting
42. **Level System** — Level up your parallel self (Explorer → Wanderer → Sage)
43. **Daily Challenges** — Complete universe-themed daily tasks
44. **Achievement Unlocks** — Unlock achievements for milestones
45. **Streak Counter** — Track daily app usage streaks
46. **Leaderboard** — Weekly most-active universe rankings
47. **Universe Inventory** — Collect virtual items for your avatar
48. **Gift System** — Send virtual gifts to other parallels

### 📊 6. Analytics & Insights (6 features)
49. **Choice Comparison** — Compare your choices vs. your parallels' choices
50. **Universe Balance** — See which life dimensions you focus on most
51. **Personality Radar** — Visual radar chart of your personality traits
52. **Activity Report** — Weekly/monthly activity summary
53. **Capsule Statistics** — How many capsules sent/received/opened
54. **Universe Popularity** — Stats on your parallel self's popularity

### ⚙️ 7. Settings & Preferences (8 features)
55. **Dark/Light Mode** — Toggle between dark cosmic and light mode
56. **Notification Settings** — Granular control over push notifications
57. **Privacy Controls** — Who can see your parallel selves
58. **Account Linking** — Link with Apple Sign In
59. **Data Export** — Export all your data
60. **Delete Account** — Full account deletion option
61. **Language Settings** — English (future: multi-language support)
62. **App Theme** — Customizable accent colors (Violet/Cyan/Amber)

### 🎨 8. Widget (3 features)
63. **Daily Parallel Quote** — Widget showing a thought from your parallel self
64. **Capsule Countdown** — Widget showing next unlockable capsule
65. **Universe of the Day** — Featured universe to explore
